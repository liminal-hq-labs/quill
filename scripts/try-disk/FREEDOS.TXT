This disk boots FreeDOS 1.3, which is free software.

KERNEL.SYS is the FreeDOS kernel (build 2043) and COMMAND.COM is FreeCOM, the FreeDOS command
interpreter, both exactly as FreeDOS 1.3 ships them. They are licensed under the GNU General
Public Licence, version 2 or (at your option) any later version. The licence text is at
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.txt and the sources are at
https://github.com/FDOS/kernel and https://github.com/FDOS/freecom. FreeDOS 1.3 itself is at
https://www.freedos.org/ and https://www.ibiblio.org/pub/micro/pc-stuff/freedos/files/distributions/1.3/official/.

QUILL\QUILLDOS.EXE is Quill, licensed Apache-2.0 OR MIT, built from
https://github.com/liminal-hq-labs/quill. It carries CWSDPMI, Charles W. Sandmann's DPMI host, unmodified.
You have the right to receive the source code of CWSDPMI and updates to it: the source is
at https://www.delorie.com/pub/djgpp/current/v2misc/csdpmi7s.zip. QUILL\QUILL.FNT is drawn from the Desk
fonts, under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 (QUILL\OFL.TXT). QUILL\EN_CA.DIC is made from SCOWL.

QUILL\CTMOUSE.EXE is CuteMouse 2.1 beta 4, FreeDOS's mouse driver, exactly as the FreeDOS 1.3 package repository
ships it, under the GNU General Public Licence, version 2. Its package, which holds its source, is at
https://www.ibiblio.org/pub/micro/pc-stuff/freedos/files/repositories/1.3/base/ctmouse.zip.
